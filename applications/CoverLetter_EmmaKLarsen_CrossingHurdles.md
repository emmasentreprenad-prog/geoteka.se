I am applying for the Geospatial Data Specialist position. End-to-end QGIS project management

My most complete example of this is the geospatial pipeline I built at the County Administrative

every processing decision in real time for reproducibility. The pipeline is public:

GeoTeka, my own consultancy, where I deliver solar potential analyses and environmental
assessments using LiDAR-derived elevation data, Sentinel-2 imagery, and QGIS from the first
dataset to the final styled map product.

Your requirements around data formats, coordinate systems, and projections are areas I work in
directly. I handle SWEREF 99 TM and WGS 84 routinely, manage reprojection and spatial reference
validation as part of data quality control, and have practical experience troubleshooting the kinds
of interoperability issues that arise when combining raster and vector sources from different
providers. Organising, cleaning, and validating spatial datasets before any analysis begins is a
habit, not an afterthought.
